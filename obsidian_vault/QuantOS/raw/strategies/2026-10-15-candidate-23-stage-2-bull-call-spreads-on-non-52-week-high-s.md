## Why this exists

Stage 1 (`docs/SURGE_DIRECTION_RESULTS.md`) PASSED on one branch. +5%
surge days in F&O stocks that are **not** fresh 52-week closing highs kept
outperforming the F&O universe: 5-day excess +0.49% (t 2.20) in mining and
+0.98% (t 3.02) in the holdout. No subset showed reversal, so only the
**bull call spread** side goes forward. Stage 1 also warned that the
effect is skewed (median 5-day excess −0.13%) and that option prices on a
surge day already include higher volatility.

Stage 2 asks: **bought at real closing option prices and sold a few days
later, net of realistic stock-option bid-ask and statutory costs, does a
bull call spread on these events make money, and which strike pair?**

**Do not edit this file after the backtest has run.**

## Events (unchanged from Stage 1)

- Every Stage 1 event (`core/surge/events.py::find_events`, same cached
  futures panel and results dates) with `breakout52 = FALSE`.
- Mining: event date before **2025-01-07** (Stage 1's cutoff). Holdout:
  from 2025-01-07. The cutoff is copied, not recomputed.

## Option data

- Cached NSE F&O bhavcopy, stock-option CE rows: legacy `OPTSTK`
  (pre-2024) and new `STO` (2024 on). Fields: expiry, strike, close,
  settlement price, contracts/volume, open interest, lot size (new format
  only).
- Underlying reference S: the stock's raw near-month futures close
  (both eras; the legacy schema carries no underlying price).

## Trade construction

- **Entry**: close of surge day t.
- **Expiry**: the nearest listed CE expiry with at least 5 trading days
  after t. (In practice the current month, or the next month inside the
  last week. Liquidity is enforced per leg below.)
- **Exit**: close of day E = min(t + 8 trading days, the last trading day
  before expiry). Stock options are physically settled, so nothing is
  held into expiry.
- **Strike grid**: the expiry's listed CE strikes on day t, in order. ATM
  = the strike nearest S_t (ties go to the lower strike).
  - long leg at ATM + k strikes, k ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3};
  - short leg w strikes above the long, w ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4}.
  - 16 structures in total, named `L{k}W{w}`. The Persistent trade
    (6100/6300 with spot 5898 on 100-point strikes) is about `L2W2`.
- **Entry validity** (per structure per event): both legs traded on t
  (contracts > 0 and close > 0), and the net debit (long close − short
  close) is > 0 and < the strike width. Otherwise that structure is
  skipped for that event, and the skip is counted.
- **Exit prices**: each leg's close on E if it traded that day. Otherwise
  NSE's settlement price for E. If no row exists for E, intrinsic value
  max(S_E − K, 0). Each fallback is counted and reported.

## P&L and costs (per share, then normalised by risk)

Gross P&L = (long_E − short_E) − debit_t. **Return on risk** = net P&L /
debit_t. Every trade risks the same capital, so trades are equally
weighted regardless of share price.

| Cost | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bid-ask | per leg, per side: max(c × leg price, ₹0.05 one tick). **Clean** c = 0 (no tick); **Base** c = 1%; **Stressed** c = 2%, about the measured stock-option round trip (1.7% median / 3.1% mean at the open, ATM, docs/GOODNIGHT_SCALPER_FEASIBILITY.md) per side. OTM legs are often wider, so Stressed is not conservative for the far strikes |
| STT | on sold premium: 0.05% to 2023-03-31, 0.0625% from 2023-04-01, 0.1% from 2024-10-01, 0.15% from 2026-04-01 |
| Exchange | 0.053% of premium turnover before 2024-10-01, 0.03503% from then |
| SEBI | 0.0001% of premium turnover |
| Stamp | 0.003% of bought premium |
| Brokerage | ₹20 per order, 4 orders per round trip, spread over the lot |
| GST | 18% on brokerage + exchange + SEBI |

Lot size: NSE's own lot for 2024 onward. Before 2024 (absent from the
legacy schema), the symbol's earliest 2024 lot is used. If there is none,
the lot is sized to ₹5,00,000 notional at S_t. This affects only the
brokerage term (disclosed).

**Stressed gates the verdict.**

## Strike-pair selection (the "picker") and verdict

1. **Choose on mining only.** For each of the 16 structures, compute
   Stressed metrics on mining trades (`core/backtest/parser.py`, return on
   risk, Sharpe annualised by observed frequency). The chosen structure is
   the one with the **highest mining Sharpe** among those with
   ≥ 100 mining trades and PF > 1. Ties go to the smaller k, then the
   smaller w. If none qualifies, Stage 2 **FAILS**.
2. **Test on the holdout**, unchanged. Stage 2 **PASSES** only if the
   chosen structure, on holdout trades under Stressed costs, has
   **≥ 30 trades, PF > 1.0 and Sharpe > 0.5**.
3. All 16 structures are reported on both mining and holdout under Clean,
   Base and Stressed. The holdout figures for the 15 unchosen structures
   are information only and cannot replace the chosen one.

Choosing 1 of 16 on mining overfits by construction. The holdout is the
only test of it.

## Also reported

- Per year and per side of the split for the chosen structure.
- Average debit as % of width, the win rate, and the median return on
  risk.
- Results-day vs non-results events for the chosen structure (descriptive
  only).
- Counts of skipped structures and exit fallbacks.

## What a PASS earns, and what it does not

A PASS earns **Stage 3**: a live probe logging real bid/ask for both legs
on live surge candidates (the evening screener can carry it) until there
are at least 30 samples. That checks the Stressed cost assumption before
any capital. A PASS here is not a capital decision.

## Known limits, stated up front

- Closing prices only. A real order trades through the spread, which is
  what the bid-ask term models, and the close is not always tradeable.
- One-day lookback for entry. The trigger needs the day's close, so live
  entry would be near 15:15 on a near-identical price.
- Futures stand in for spot in strike selection.
- The selection-per-event "expected value picker" discussed with the user
  is NOT tested here. If a fixed structure passes, that is Stage 2b, with
  its own pre-registration.
