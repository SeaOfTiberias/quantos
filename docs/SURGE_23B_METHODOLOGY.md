# Candidate 23b — Wide Bull Call Spreads on Surges, Forward Paper Test with Real Quotes: Methodology, Pre-Committed 2026-10-15 Before Any Forward Data Exists

## Why this exists

Candidate 23 Stage 2 FAILED (`docs/SURGE_SPREAD_STAGE2_RESULTS.md`): no
bull call spread structure cleared Stressed PF 1 on the 2020–24 mining
set, and with stale prints removed none cleared PF 1 even at the Base
(measured) cost. But in the 2025–26 holdout, wide structures made money
at Base cost (cleaned: L2W4 PF 1.18, Sharpe 1.10; L0W4 PF 1.14). That
matches Stage 1, where the holdout drift (+0.98%) was double the
mining drift (+0.49%).

That pattern was found by looking at 16 structures on the holdout, so it
cannot be confirmed on the same data. It is either a real change in how
surges follow through, or the luck of the best of 16. Only **new data**
can separate the two. New data can also replace the one assumption every
Stage 2 number rests on, the bid-ask cost, with **real quotes**.

**Do not edit this file after the first forward trade is logged.**

## Part A — Forward paper test (the verdict)

### The structure, fixed now

**L2W4 only**: long call 2 listed strikes above ATM, short call 4 listed
strikes above the long. Chosen because it was the strongest wide
structure in the cleaned 2025–26 holdout. Fixing one structure today means
the forward data cannot be used to pick another later. L0W4 is not
traded or reported as an alternative.

### Daily process (read-only; never places an order)

Runs once each NSE trading day at **15:15 IST** (`scripts/run_surge_23b.py`):

1. **Universe**: every F&O stock in the latest published F&O bhavcopy (STF
   symbols).
2. **Trigger**: Fyers quote change vs the previous close ≥ **+5.0%** at
   15:15. Stage 1 used the futures close-to-close; the live trigger uses
   spot at 15:15 (disclosed difference).
3. **Not a fresh 52-week high**: the 15:15 price is below the maximum
   daily close of the prior 252 sessions (Fyers daily history).
   Otherwise, skip.
4. **Cooldown**: no new entry in a symbol within 10 trading days of its
   last logged entry (as in Stage 1).
5. **Expiry**: the nearest listed expiry with at least 5 weekdays after
   today, counting the expiry day. (`core/reference/calendar.py` is derived
   from past bhavcopies and cannot see future holidays, so a holiday can
   make this one session shorter. Amended 2026-10-15, before any forward
   trade.)
6. **Strikes**: from the Fyers option chain, ATM = the listed strike
   nearest the underlying's price in the chain response; long = ATM + 2
   strikes, short = long + 4 strikes.
7. **Entry is valid** only if both legs show a two-sided quote (bid > 0
   and ask > 0) and the real debit (long ask − short bid) is > 0 and less
   than the strike width. Otherwise the candidate is logged as skipped,
   with the reason.
8. **Paper entry**: buy the long leg at its **ask**, sell the short at its
   **bid**. Mid prices are logged alongside.
9. **Paper exit**: at 15:15 on the first run where either 8 sessions
   have elapsed since entry, or the expiry is at most 1 weekday away (the
   last session before expiry). A session counts only if NIFTY 50 has a
   daily bar dated that day, so holidays are never counted. Sell the long at its bid, buy the
   short at its ask. If a leg has no two-sided quote at exit, use its last
   traded price and flag the trade.

### Costs and return

Net P&L per share = (long bid_exit − short ask_exit) − (long ask_entry −
short bid_entry) − statutory costs (`core/surge/spreads.py::statutory_cost`,
using the lot size from the chain response). The bid-ask cost is real
because the fills are at the quotes. **Return on risk** = net P&L / real
entry debit.

### Verdict

Taken once **at least 60 closed trades** exist. The recent rate of about
25 qualifying surges a month gives roughly 2½–3 months, but it is not
time-boxed.

**PASS requires all of:**

1. profit factor > 1.0 and Sharpe > 0.5 on return on risk
   (`core/backtest/parser.py`, annualised by observed frequency);
2. mean return on risk > 0 with a t-stat ≥ 1.5, clustered by entry date.

A PASS earns a capital conversation with the user, nothing automatic.
A FAIL closes the surge-spread family.

**No peeking**: until 60 closed trades exist, interim checks may confirm
the log is accumulating (counts, no malformed rows), but must not report
P&L.

## Part B — Market-state log (descriptive only)

Each daily run also records one row of market context:

- NIFTY 50 change %;
- India VIX;
- F&O breadth (share of F&O stocks up on the day);
- the number of ≥ +5% surges.

Every logged trade carries that day's row. When the verdict is taken, P&L
is reported split by these states, **for information only**. No state
gates anything unless Part C passes.

## Part C — Historical persistence check (run now, before Part A starts)

**Question**: does recent surge follow-through predict next month's? If
it does, a live "follow-through is currently positive" gate is
legitimate. If not, the 2025–26 jump cannot be timed in advance.

- **Events**: Stage 1 events with `breakout52 = FALSE` (same code and
  cache), **mining period only: 2020-05 to 2024-12**. 2025–26 is excluded
  because its level shift is already known and would create apparent
  persistence.
- **Monthly series**: for each calendar month m, outcome = the mean X(5)
  of that month's events. Predictor = the mean X(5) of events from months
  m−3..m−1 whose 5-day outcome was known before m's first trading day.
  This needs ≥ 15 such events; months without them are dropped.
- **PASS requires both**:
  1. Spearman correlation(predictor, outcome) > 0 with one-sided p < 0.05;
  2. the mean outcome in months with predictor > 0 exceeds the mean in
     months with predictor ≤ 0.
- **If PASS**: Part A adds a **secondary arm**. Each trade records whether
  the trailing follow-through of the **forward-logged** surges (their
  realised 5-day stock return, last 3 months, ≥ 15 trades) was positive
  at entry. At the verdict, gated and ungated results are both reported.
  The primary verdict stays ungated.
- **If FAIL**: market state stays descriptive only (Part B), and no gate
  is added.

Known weakness: about 56 monthly points gives low power. A FAIL here
means "no evidence of persistence", not "proven no persistence".

## What would make this untrustworthy after the fact

- Switching from L2W4 to another structure after seeing forward results.
- Reporting P&L before 60 closed trades, or stopping early on a good
  run.
- Using mid prices instead of the real quotes for the verdict.
- Promoting a Part B state to a gate without Part C having passed.
- Back-filling missed days from bhavcopy closes into the verdict sample.
  Missed days are disclosed, not filled.
