# Call-plan replay: the 194 F&O screener calls, 9 Oct – 13 Nov 2026

**Run 2026-11-15 on the VM** (code e5823be, `scripts/call_plans.py --replay-from 2026-10-09 --start 2026-10-09`, scratch root
`~/.quantos/call_plans_replay_fno_20261009`, no Telegram, no API, live state untouched). Darvas picks not included (only the
latest scan's file exists). Each call is planned from the card as of its own close and walked bar by bar with the version made
the evening before (no look-ahead). Not a pre-registered test: a check that the code reads the chart the way the user does.

**Caveats:** one 5-week stretch; 194 calls on only 79 stocks (TCS, CANBK, SBILIFE, TATAPOWER 6 each), so the counts overlap;
"as sent" = spot change from the call to the 13 Nov close, signed for the side (not option P&L).

## Defects found

1. **The "ran without us" lapse fires on noise.** The sweep plan's target (first opposing zone) sat a median **0.24 ATR**
   from the call's price (94 of 103 under 1 ATR), so the first wiggle "reached the target" and killed the plan before any
   sweep. Two thirds of those calls then went against the call (SBILIFE PE 9 Oct: target 1652.9 touched 14 Oct, stock
   ended 1789.9, +6% against). The lapse rule, or the target choice, has to measure from the sweep entry, not the price.
2. **Stops beyond the sweep are too tight.** Sweep-track risk median **0.61 ATR** (1.8%); 7 of 47 under 0.5 ATR; LICI
   30 Oct 0.04 ATR. BEL 9 Oct: extreme 375.2, stop 375.99 (0.38 ATR), stopped the day it entered.
3. **Breakout entries need wide stops** (median 1.9 ATR, 5.2%), so most fail the R:R ≥ 1 gate (64 of 194 skipped).

## GLAND pattern ("stopped on the first sweep, the real sweep came deeper")

Of 22 stopped tracks: **3** went > 0.5 ATR beyond the stop and then reached the target (BEL 9 Oct; RADICO 12 and 14 Oct,
one stock). **1** was stopped and then reached the target without going deeper (MPHASIS 2 Nov, a shake-out). **15** went
deeper and never reached the target: the call was wrong (mostly PE calls into the late-October rally) and the stop did its
job. 3 stopped and neither. Not tuned for; counted per the 11-14 note.

## What the map did right

The 36 calls it never entered lost −4.0% on average as sent (8 of 36 went the call's way): the R:R gate and "the level
broke" kept it out of the worst calls (BANKINDIA PE −18%, UNITDSPR PE −15%, TATACONSUM PE −15%).
